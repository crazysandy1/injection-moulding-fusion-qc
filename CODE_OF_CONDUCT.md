# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want participation to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and welcoming. Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Assume good faith. Focus on what is best for the project.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, trolling.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing other people's private information without permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they judge inappropriate.

Report problems privately through [GitHub private reporting](https://github.com/crazysandy1/injection-moulding-fusion-qc/security/advisories/new) or by contacting the maintainer [@crazysandy1](https://github.com/crazysandy1) via GitHub. All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

The full enforcement guidelines and attribution are in the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
